# XYZ-492 Troubleshooting Quick Guide

Source: https://support.example-daikin.test/xyz-492/troubleshooting

## F3 control board failure
Symptom: unit powers on, compressor never starts, fan runs. Most likely the inverter PCB. Confirm with the self-test, then replace PCB-492 (with CAP-492). Needs 'daikin-inverter' skill. Offer a loaner if PCB-492 is out of stock at the serving depot.

## F6 temperature sensor
Symptom: erratic temperature regulation. Replace the outdoor sensor; a standard visit, no board work and no scarce parts.

## U4 communication error
Symptom: indoor and outdoor units disagree. Inspect the interconnect harness first; do not replace a board until the harness is ruled out.
