# Field Technician SOP - Inverter Board Repair

Source: https://sop.example-fieldflow.test/inverter-board

## Before travel
Confirm the required skill ('daikin-inverter') and that the serving location holds the part (PCB-492). If stock is uncertain, reserve before dispatch to avoid a wasted visit.

## High-voltage safety
Always discharge CAP-492 before touching the board. If a safety risk is reported on site, stop and escalate for human approval before proceeding.

## Completion
Run the self-test, record the fault code cleared, and close the work order to generate the service report.
